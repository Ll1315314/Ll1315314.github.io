# About

I'm a full-stack engineer working mainly with **Java (Spring Boot) + Vue**. Lately my focus is on **putting LLMs into real products** and on **data collection — cost-cutting and anti-bot research**.

More than "which frameworks I know," I care about two things:

1. **Owning something end to end** — backend, frontend, deployment, and debugging — all the way to production.
2. **Evidence-backed decisions** — proving with data where cost can be cut, and explaining with experiments where a path genuinely can't be taken. A well-evidenced "no" matters as much as a successful "yes."

## What I'm into right now

- Reliable LLM agents / tool-use in production systems (safety boundaries, cost control, hallucination mitigation)
- Engineering multimodal models into review and monitoring workflows
- The network-layer fundamentals behind data collection, anti-bot and risk control
